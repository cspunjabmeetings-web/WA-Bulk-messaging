🛡️ Keep your number safe

  • Message only people who know you / opted in. Reports & blocks are the #1 ban reason.
  • New number? Start with 50–100/day for the first week, then increase slowly.
  • Use {name} and personal text – identical mass texts are flagged faster.
  • Trial plan of WasenderAPI allows only 1 msg/min & 50/day. Paid plans are much higher.
  • "Account Protection" in WasenderAPI limits to 1 msg / 5 sec – keep min delay ≥ 5s if enabled.
  • Always add an opt-out line, e.g. "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" – and add those numbers to the Blocklist.
